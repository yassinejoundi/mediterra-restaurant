## Business Overview

Mediterra Restaurant is an Italian restaurant in Marrakech. The website should present Mediterra as a premium yet warm dining destination for guests looking for Italian food, Mediterranean atmosphere, and an elegant dinner experience.

The restaurant should be positioned around authentic Italian food with Mediterranean warmth, including handmade pasta, pizza, refined Italian classics, fresh ingredients, and an overall premium dinner experience.

The website is a new build. No existing website, domain, or hosting details have been confirmed yet.

## Target Audience

The primary audience is tourists and couples looking for a premium Italian dining experience in Marrakech. The site should make it easy for them to understand the atmosphere, trust the restaurant, view the menu, locate the venue, and reserve a table.

Secondary audiences include local residents and expats in Marrakech who want an elegant but welcoming Italian restaurant for dinner, dates, or social occasions.

Audience needs and motivations:
- Find a restaurant that feels premium, romantic, and reliable.
- See enough visual atmosphere and menu detail before deciding.
- Reserve easily without friction.
- Contact the restaurant by WhatsApp or phone if they have questions before booking.
- Trust that the dining experience will match a premium night out in Marrakech.

Likely objections to address:
- Whether the restaurant is genuinely premium and worth choosing.
- Whether the food, atmosphere, and service match tourist expectations.
- Whether reservation and location details are clear.
- Whether guests can easily ask questions before reserving.
