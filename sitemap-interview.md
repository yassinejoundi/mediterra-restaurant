## Known Inputs

### Business Goals

- Primary goal: drive table reservations.
- Primary CTA: reservation form.
- Secondary CTAs: view menu, WhatsApp, phone call, get directions, Instagram.
- Website is a new premium restaurant website, not a redesign.
- Launch language: English only.

### Audience

- Primary audience: tourist couples visiting Marrakech who want a romantic, reliable, premium Italian dinner.
- Secondary audience: local residents, expats, and groups of friends seeking a beautiful social dinner and shared dishes.
- Strategic referral audiences: hotel staff, riad hosts, concierges, and recommendation-makers.

### Offer

- Premium Italian dining in Marrakech.
- Core menu direction: handmade pasta, pizza, refined Italian classics, fresh Mediterranean ingredients, desserts and drinks.
- Menu and review content may be placeholder content until real details are available.

### Positioning

- Mediterra is a romantic Mediterranean table in Marrakech where handmade Italian food, coastal ease, and generous hospitality create a beautiful evening with real substance.
- Core positioning: beauty with substance; personal, not staged.
- Competitive frame: premium-night-out options in Marrakech, especially rooftop restaurants, trendy Instagram restaurants, riad restaurants, and visually driven tourist venues.
- Differentiator: intimacy over spectacle, handmade Italian craft, personal hospitality, and romantic Mediterranean atmosphere.

### Brand Voice And Experience

- Editorial, atmospheric, poetic, romantic, and grounded in concrete details.
- Avoid stiff fine-dining language, Instagrammable claims, loud luxury, generic tourism language, and unsupported superiority claims.
- Visual direction: coastal Italy with Marrakech riad warmth; warm ivory, olive, terracotta, deep wine, espresso/charcoal, candlelight, linen, plaster, and tactile Mediterranean materials.

## Strategic Insights

- The site should be reservation-led, but menu viewing is the most important supporting path before booking.
- The homepage must quickly create emotional desire and visual trust before asking for a reservation.
- Tourist couples need reassurance that the restaurant is beautiful in reality, not only online.
- Reviews, photography, menu clarity, location clarity, and contact options are conversion infrastructure, not decoration.
- WhatsApp and phone should reduce anxiety without competing with the reservation form.
- The website should sell the evening, not only the food.
- Google search is expected to be the most common first touchpoint, so the site needs SEO-ready page architecture and landing sections that immediately validate search intent.
- Core decision triggers: romantic atmosphere, strong photos, menu clarity, reviews, easy location, and a simple reservation path.
- First version will use premium generated imagery until real restaurant photography is ready.

## Interview Log

### Phase 1 — Website Objectives

Question: Besides table reservations, what is the most important secondary business goal the website should support?

Answer: Convert tourist couples into reservations while making Mediterra easy to trust, recommend, and find in Marrakech searches.

Strategic insight: The website must balance direct conversion with trust-building and SEO/referral credibility. The architecture should not only push reservations; it should give visitors and recommendation-makers enough proof to confidently choose or refer Mediterra.

Decision: Reservation-first website with trust, recommendation, and Marrakech search visibility as explicit secondary objectives.

### Phase 2 — Customer Journey

Question: What do you expect will be the most common first touchpoint for tourist couples discovering Mediterra?

Answer: Google search.

Strategic insight: Search visitors will arrive with comparison intent and limited patience. They need immediate confirmation of cuisine, location, atmosphere, credibility, menu quality, and reservation availability.

Decision: Treat organic search as the primary acquisition path for tourist couples.

Question: What is the strongest reason a tourist couple should choose Mediterra over another romantic restaurant in Marrakech?

Answer: Romantic atmosphere, strong photos, menu clarity, reviews, easy location, and a simple reservation path.

Strategic insight: The website should not rely on a single differentiator. It should stack evidence in the exact order a tourist couple needs it: emotional attraction, visual proof, food confidence, social proof, logistical clarity, then booking ease.

Decision: The conversion path must repeatedly support six decision triggers: atmosphere, photography, menu clarity, reviews, location, and reservation simplicity.

### Phase 3 — Website Type

Question: Should the website be designed primarily as a simple restaurant booking site, or as a premium hospitality brand site with richer story, atmosphere, and SEO sections supporting reservations?

Answer: Premium hospitality brand site with richer story, atmosphere, and SEO sections supporting reservations.

Strategic insight: The site should not be a thin menu-and-contact website. It needs enough emotional atmosphere, brand story, search relevance, and visual proof to make Mediterra feel like a considered dining destination.

Decision: Use a premium hospitality architecture with reservation-led conversion, rich atmospheric storytelling, and SEO-aware content sections.

### Phase 4 — Page Discovery

Question: Should `Romantic Dinner in Marrakech` be included as a first-launch page, or kept as a later SEO expansion?

Answer: The pages included will be Home, Menu, Reservations, Story / About Us, Gallery, Contact, with FAQ, Terms, and Privacy as secondary pages.

Strategic insight: The first-launch architecture should stay focused and premium, avoiding unnecessary page sprawl. SEO intent for romantic dining, Italian restaurant Marrakech, and tourist dinner searches should be supported through the Home, Story, Menu, Gallery, FAQ, and Contact pages rather than a separate landing page at launch.

Decision: Approved launch page set: Home, Menu, Reservations, Story / About Us, Gallery, Contact, FAQ, Terms, and Privacy. Dedicated SEO landing pages are deferred.

### Phase 5 — Conversion Strategy

Question: Should the reservation form work as a request-based form where the restaurant confirms manually, or as an instant confirmed booking?

Answer: Use the recommended approach.

Recommendation applied: Request-based reservation form with manual confirmation.

Strategic insight: Because real booking capacity, table availability, form destination, and reservation operations are not confirmed, instant confirmation would create operational risk. A request-based flow protects the restaurant while still making the guest path simple.

Decision: Reservation form should submit a reservation request, then the restaurant confirms manually by preferred contact method. Website copy should set expectation clearly, such as "Request your table and our team will confirm your reservation."

Question: Where should reservation requests be sent at launch: email, WhatsApp, both, or not decided yet?

Answer: WhatsApp.

Strategic insight: WhatsApp is familiar, fast, and well-suited for tourists in Marrakech. The reservation page should reduce manual typing by turning form details into a structured WhatsApp message when possible.

Decision: Reservation requests should be routed through WhatsApp at launch.

### Phase 6 — Content Strategy

Question: For the first website version, should we plan around real restaurant photos being available, or premium placeholder/generated imagery until real photos are ready?

Answer: Premium generated imagery until real photos are ready.

Strategic insight: Generated imagery can support early design and launch, but final conversion quality will improve significantly with real photography. The sitemap should specify image categories so generated visuals and future photo shoots serve the same strategic roles.

Decision: Launch content plan may use premium generated imagery, with real food, interior, guest, and welcome-ritual photography listed as required future replacement assets.

Question: Should the first sitemap assume a full draft menu with invented placeholder dishes and prices, or menu categories only without dish-level detail?

Answer: Full draft menu with invented placeholder dishes and prices.

Strategic insight: Dish-level detail will make the Menu page more convincing for tourists comparing restaurants. Placeholder prices also help page layout and decision confidence, but they must be treated as draft content until the real menu is confirmed.

Decision: The Menu page should be architected around a full draft menu with invented placeholder dishes and prices, clearly marked internally as replaceable content.

### Phase 7 — SEO Strategy Inputs

Question: Which single SEO search intent should be treated as the top priority for the website launch?

Answer: Italian restaurant Marrakech.

Strategic insight: The core SEO architecture should first make Mediterra clearly relevant for people searching for an Italian restaurant in Marrakech, then support romantic dining and tourist dinner intent through page copy, FAQs, menu sections, and metadata.

Decision: Primary launch SEO intent: Italian restaurant Marrakech.

### Architecture Approval

Question: Do you approve this page architecture as the final launch sitemap direction?

Answer: Yes.

Strategic insight: The approved architecture is focused enough for launch while still supporting premium positioning, trust-building, reservation conversion, and Google search relevance.

Decision: Proceed to final sitemap with the approved launch architecture.
