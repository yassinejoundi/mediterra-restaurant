# Stitch Theme

## Project Name

Mediterra Restaurant

## Theme Name

Boutique Mediterranean Hospitality

## Theme Description

A warm premium Mediterranean restaurant theme with romantic editorial hospitality, coastal Italian ease, and Marrakech riad warmth. The experience should feel intimate, tactile, composed, and quietly premium, with breathable editorial spacing, graceful serif headlines, clean practical body typography, sun-warmed plaster backgrounds, deep wine reservation intent, olive and terracotta material accents, softly rounded shapes, and whisper-soft candlelit elevation. Interactions should feel like a gracious invitation to the table rather than a loud sales prompt.

## Visual Atmosphere

Romantic Mediterranean table in Marrakech; intimate, warm, editorial, tactile, candlelit, and restrained. The mood is sun-warmed by day and softly glowing by night, balancing coastal Italian ease with Marrakech warmth. The brand should feel beautiful with substance, personal rather than staged, premium without stiffness, and sensory without excess.

## Color Mode

Light with dark accent sections.

## Typography

### Heading Font

Playfair Display

### Body Font

Inter

### Font Personality

Headlines should feel graceful, editorial, romantic, and composed, with high contrast and restrained weight. Body typography should feel clean, modern, calm, and highly readable for tourists scanning menu, reservation, location, FAQ, and contact information on mobile.

### Hierarchy Notes

Build hierarchy through scale, rhythm, whitespace, and relaxed line breaks rather than aggressive weight. Use regular or medium headline weights, regular body copy, and medium labels/navigation for clarity. Small labels may use subtle tracking; large romantic headlines should avoid excessive spacing.

## Shape Language

Soft, tactile, and composed. Use pill-like primary buttons, gently rounded content containers, refined image corners, and soft panel transitions. Avoid sharp corporate geometry, overly playful curves, heavy decorative frames, or harsh boxed layouts.

## Roundness

Softly rounded, mapped to Stitch `ROUND_TWELVE`.

## Density

Balanced, leaning spacious in atmosphere and brand sections. Menu, reservation, and FAQ sections may become more structured but should remain clear and breathable.

## Spacing Personality

Generous editorial whitespace with intentional pauses around important imagery and copy. Rhythm should alternate between immersive atmospheric moments and quiet informational panels. Spacing should feel hosted, not empty; composed, not sparse.

## Shadow & Elevation

Whisper-soft. Depth should feel natural, like candlelight or warm afternoon light, not digital elevation. Avoid strong floating-card shadows, high contrast depth, and generic SaaS-style shadows.

## Component Personality

### Buttons

Primary buttons should use Deep Candlelit Wine with Warm Ivory Plaster text, softly pill-like shape, generous width, and comfortable touch size. They should feel decisive, romantic, and premium. Secondary buttons should be quieter, using transparent or linen-toned surfaces, fine borders, Espresso Charcoal text, and subtle olive or terracotta active cues.

### Cards

Cards should feel like printed menus, linen-backed notes, or refined table cards. Use Warm Ivory Plaster or Natural Linen Cream surfaces, fine low-contrast edges, softly rounded corners, and whisper-soft depth. Avoid dense grids of identical boxes.

### Navigation

Navigation should feel composed, easy, and reservation-led. The wordmark should be prominent but not oversized. Links should be minimal and calm, with the reservation action clearly emphasized without making the header busy or heavy.

### Forms

Forms should feel reassuring and personal. Use spacious warm-toned fields, clear calm labels, fine espresso-tinted borders, and Olive Grove Green or Deep Candlelit Wine active states. Helper text should make reservation uncertainty clear and hospitable.

## Do Rules

Use warm ivory plaster as the environmental foundation.

Use deep wine for reservation intent and romantic depth.

Use olive for freshness, hospitality, and calm cues.

Use terracotta sparingly for craft and material warmth.

Use brass only as a discreet premium highlight.

Use graceful serif headlines and clean sans body text.

Use generous editorial spacing and disciplined but warm grids.

Use soft rounded shapes and whisper-soft shadows.

Keep reservation access clear and calm.

Make every visual decision feel personal, tactile, and credible.

## Don't Rules

Do not create pages, screens, layouts, components, wireframes, mockups, HTML, CSS, or page prompts from this theme step.

Do not use glossy black-and-gold luxury language.

Do not make the brand nightclub-like, overly Moroccanized, generic rustic Italian, corporate, sterile, or tourist-brochure polished.

Do not use harsh shadows, bright cold colors, aggressive hover effects, heavy ornament, or decorative frames.

Do not make the experience feel performative, influencer-led, or built around a single photo moment.

Do not overuse brass/gold.

Do not make typography theatrical, script-like, overly rustic, or fashion-cold.
