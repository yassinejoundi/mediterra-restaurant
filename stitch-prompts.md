# Stitch Prompts: Mediterra Website

Use these prompts to generate high-fidelity Stitch screens for the Mediterra sitemap. Each prompt is written for a responsive web page, desktop-first with strong mobile clarity.

## Global Design System

Apply this design system to every screen unless a page prompt says otherwise.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**

- Platform: Responsive web, desktop-first, mobile-optimized for tourists deciding quickly
- Brand: Mediterra, a premium Italian restaurant in Marrakech
- Theme: Light, warm, romantic, editorial, tactile, quietly premium
- Visual Atmosphere: Warm premium Mediterranean restaurant, coastal Italian ease with Marrakech riad warmth, candlelit but not dramatic, intimate but not exclusive, beautiful with substance
- Background: Warm Ivory Plaster (#F7F0E4) for main page backgrounds and spacious editorial sections
- Surface: Natural Linen Cream (#EFE2CF) for menu cards, reservation form panels, quote areas, and soft content containers
- Primary Accent: Deep Candlelit Wine (#5A1F2B) for reservation CTAs, conversion areas, and romantic depth
- Secondary Accent: Olive Grove Green (#6F7651) for active navigation, small labels, category markers, proof points, and hospitality cues
- Warm Detail: Soft Terracotta Clay (#B86F4B) for restrained dividers, dish prices, image captions, and story accents
- Premium Detail: Muted Brass Glow (#C29A5B) only for tiny highlights such as review stars, fine rules, candlelight accents, and small decorative details
- Text Primary: Espresso Charcoal (#2A211D) for headlines, body copy, navigation, and strong contrast
- Text Secondary: Candle Smoke Taupe (#8A7A69) for captions, helper text, metadata, address details, and secondary navigation
- Support Tint: Soft Sage Mist (#D8D7C2) for gentle badges, gallery filters, atmospheric washes, and quiet proof areas
- Typography: Graceful editorial serif for the Mediterra wordmark and large headlines, paired with a clean modern sans serif for body copy, menus, forms, FAQs, and practical information
- Buttons: Primary buttons are softly pill-like, Deep Candlelit Wine (#5A1F2B) with Warm Ivory Plaster (#F7F0E4) text; secondary buttons are linen-toned or transparent with fine borders and Espresso Charcoal (#2A211D) text
- Cards: Linen or ivory surfaces with fine low-contrast borders, softly rounded corners, whisper-soft natural shadows, and generous spacing; avoid generic SaaS card grids
- Forms: Warm-toned, spacious, mobile-friendly inputs with clear labels, espresso-tinted borders, and active states using Olive Grove Green (#6F7651) or Deep Candlelit Wine (#5A1F2B)
- Imagery: Warm editorial documentary photography direction with candlelit tables, handmade pasta, olive oil, bread, linen, ceramic, dark wood, warm plaster, soft human presence, and believable restaurant atmosphere
- Layout: Editorial hospitality with generous whitespace, asymmetric image-and-copy pairings, disciplined warm grids, immersive atmospheric imagery, calm navigation, and reservation-led hierarchy
- Avoid: Glossy black-and-gold luxury, nightclub mood, generic rustic Italian cliches, overly ornate Moroccan styling, sterile corporate minimalism, over-staged influencer moments, harsh shadows, bright digital colors

## Home Page Prompt

A romantic, reservation-led homepage for Mediterra that immediately communicates premium Italian dining in Marrakech, handmade craft, warm hospitality, and an evening that feels personal rather than staged.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):** Use the Global Design System above.

**Page Structure:**

1. **Header Navigation:** Elegant Mediterra serif wordmark on the left, calm navigation links for Home, Menu, Story, Gallery, Contact, and a visually emphasized pill-shaped Reserve a Table button on the right. On mobile, keep reservation access prominent.
2. **Hero Section:** Full-width immersive editorial hero with a romantic candlelit interior or intimate Mediterranean table in Marrakech. Add a concise headline: "A romantic Mediterranean table in Marrakech." Include supporting copy about handmade Italian food and generous hospitality, a location cue, a primary Reserve a Table CTA, and a secondary View Menu CTA.
3. **Trust Snapshot:** Three or four refined proof points in a breathable row or asymmetric card layout: Handmade Pasta, Romantic Dining Room, Mediterranean Warmth, WhatsApp Reservations. Include a quiet View Gallery link.
4. **Menu Preview:** Linen-toned menu-card section with featured placeholder dishes, short descriptions, prices, category links, and warm food imagery. Prioritize handmade pasta, Italian classics, pizza, desserts, and drinks. Include Explore the Menu CTA.
5. **Atmosphere / Gallery Preview:** Curated editorial image grid showing candlelight, warm plaster, linen, olive tones, intimate tables, pasta, welcome moment, couple dining, and shared dishes. Use varied image proportions, not a generic uniform grid. Include View Gallery CTA.
6. **Story Teaser:** Asymmetric image-and-copy section with the message: "Born from friendship, Italian discipline, and Marrakech generosity." Keep copy restrained and human without founder names. Include Read Our Story CTA.
7. **Reviews / Guest Words:** Placeholder review cards styled like intimate table notes. Include review snippets about atmosphere, handmade food, hospitality, and a dinner worth remembering. Make clear these are placeholder-style design content, not verified reviews. Include Reserve a Table CTA.
8. **Location Preview:** Practical but warm section with address placeholder, map preview area, opening hours placeholder, WhatsApp and phone links, and a Get Directions CTA.
9. **Final Reservation CTA:** Deep Candlelit Wine or Espresso Charcoal closing invitation with a candlelit table image, manual WhatsApp confirmation reassurance, and one strong Reserve a Table button.
10. **Footer:** Warm editorial footer with footer navigation, Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Maps, address placeholder, hours placeholder, and reservation reminder.

## Reservations Page Prompt

8. **Footer:** Footer with contact, hours, WhatsApp, Google Maps, and legal links.
