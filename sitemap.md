# Website Strategy Summary

Mediterra's website is a premium hospitality brand site built to convert Google search visitors, especially tourist couples, into reservation requests. The site must make Mediterra easy to discover, trust, recommend, and book as an Italian restaurant in Marrakech.

The architecture is reservation-led, but not thin or transactional. It sells the evening first: romantic Mediterranean atmosphere, handmade Italian food, warm hospitality, credible visuals, menu clarity, reviews, location confidence, and a simple WhatsApp-based reservation request.

Primary strategic direction: convert tourist couples into reservations while making Mediterra easy to trust, recommend, and find in Marrakech searches.

# Website Goals

# Conversion Strategy

## Booking Model

Reservations should use a request-based flow with manual confirmation by WhatsApp.

This avoids operational risk because table availability, booking capacity, and reservation systems are not confirmed yet. Copy should clearly state that the form sends a reservation request, not an instant confirmed booking.

## Reservation Flow

1. Visitor clicks `Reserve a Table`.
2. Visitor completes a short reservation request form.
3. Form details generate or submit a structured WhatsApp message.
4. Restaurant confirms manually through WhatsApp.

## Reservation Form Fields

- Full name
- Phone / WhatsApp
- Email, optional
- Date
- Time
- Number of guests
- Special requests
- Preferred contact method

## Conversion Principles

- Keep `Reserve a Table` visible in the global header.
- Place reservation CTAs after each major trust-building moment.
- Use `View Menu` as the main secondary action before reservation.
- Use WhatsApp and phone as reassurance options, not competing primary CTAs.
- Repeat location and contact clarity near decision points.

# Internal Linking Strategy

- Home should link to Gallery after atmosphere proof, Story after brand proof, and Contact after logistical proof.

- Footer should expose all pages, social/contact links, and map access.

## Page-Level SEO Roles

- Home: primary SEO entry for Italian restaurant Marrakech and premium Italian dining in Marrakech.
- Menu: supports Italian menu, handmade pasta, pizza, and dinner search intent.
- Reservations: supports restaurant reservation Marrakech and booking intent.
- Story: supports brand credibility, Italian craft, and Marrakech hospitality.
- Gallery: supports atmosphere, romantic restaurant, and visual trust intent.
- Contact: supports location, directions, hours, and local discovery.
- FAQ: supports long-tail questions and rich snippet opportunities.

## SEO Notes

- Avoid unsupported claims such as "best Italian restaurant in Marrakech" in visible copy.
- Metadata may target competitive intent carefully without making unverifiable claims.
- Dedicated SEO landing pages such as `Romantic Dinner in Marrakech` are deferred until after launch.
